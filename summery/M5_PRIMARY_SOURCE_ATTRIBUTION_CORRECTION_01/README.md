# M5_PRIMARY_SOURCE_ATTRIBUTION_CORRECTION_01

**Date:** 2026-10-01
**Type:** primary-source attribution correction; no biological or model outcomes were recomputed.

## Finding

The earlier M5 summary attributed mouse delay-eyeblink findings to Kimpo et al. (2014), DOI `10.7554/eLife.02076`. The primary eLife record identifies that study as *Gating of neural error signals during motor learning* and describes vestibulo-ocular reflex (VOR) learning. Silva et al. (2024), DOI `10.1038/s41593-024-01594-7`, is the primary source for the delay-eyeblink claims in this project.

Silva et al. report that optogenetic climbing-fiber (CF) stimulation can substitute for the airpuff US, that learned response timing shifts with the CS–stimulation interval, and that optogenetic inferior-olive inhibition during the airpuff US blocks learning while preserving the unconditioned reflex. The latter contrast involved four mice per group. The study does not identify a unique synaptic eligibility-trace equation.

## Correction and boundary

The active M5 results narrative now carries this correction explicitly. The synthetic benchmark outcomes and their classification are unchanged. Frozen M5/M7 contracts retain their original text as historical records; read them with this source correction. No M5/M7 experiment was reopened.
