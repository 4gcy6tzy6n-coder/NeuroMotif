# Project-wide venue-fit decision 01

**Date checked:** 2026-10-01  
**Scope:** current full-project evidence report and Route 2 report, assessed against official journal descriptions available on this date.  
**Decision:** no external journal is presently supported as a submission target. Keep NMI as a long-term target, but do not draft or submit this evidence portfolio as an NMI Article/Analysis or PLOS Computational Biology Research/Methods article on the current record. Neural Computation has thematic scope overlap; that alone does not establish contribution readiness.

## Why a venue decision is needed

The full-project report is now complete as an internal synthesis, but the original four-result positive chain is not established. The Fish1.5 within-specimen analysis did not support the positive recurrence–persistence association, and its frozen topology-null inference is invalid/indeterminate. Cross-system convergence is not established; the public worm reanalysis is blocked by schema; and the existing M2 synthetic tests do not establish a robust motif-specific AI advantage. M0–M10 do not share one estimand or a common independent unit, so they cannot be collapsed into one pooled project effect. These are constraints on the contribution, not just formatting gaps.

## Official journal criteria and project fit

| Venue | Official scope / format threshold | Fit to current evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nature Machine Intelligence — Article** | A “substantial novel research study,” often involving a complex story across techniques or approaches. | The project has a series of bounded and heterogeneous tests, but no validated biological motif, cross-system result, or demonstrated motif-specific AI advance. | **NOT READY.** |
| **Nature Machine Intelligence — Analysis** | A new analysis of existing data or comparative analysis leading to novel, arresting conclusions important to a broad audience. | E1 is a one-specimen retrospective non-supportive association with an invalid frozen null; several cross-system arms are blocked or resolution-limited; synthetic experiments have different tasks and are outcome-informed. A broad, field-changing conclusion is not currently established. | **NOT READY.** |
| **PLOS Computational Biology — Research / Methods** | Originality, innovation, high importance, significant biological and/or methodological insight, rigorous methods, and substantial evidence. Methods/software papers should have exceptional importance and potential for broad adoption. | The biological contribution is not independently established, and the tested computational abstractions have not shown a sufficiently general advantage. The existing analysis workflow is not an evaluated general method. | **NOT READY.** |
| **Neural Computation** | Scope includes theory, modeling, computation and statistics in neuroscience and neurally inspired information processing systems. | The topic fits in broad terms, but the evidence portfolio has not yielded a single strong new computational result or a validated biological computation. | **SCOPE OVERLAP ONLY; CONTRIBUTION NOT READY.** |

These are readiness decisions, not predictions of editorial decisions. A journal's willingness to consider negative, mixed, or retrospective evidence does not replace the need for a coherent, original contribution.

## Most defensible current product

The current deliverable is an internal, evidence-bounded project report. It documents that the proposed positive chain is not established and distinguishes unsupported primary associations, invalid null inference, blocked tests, and mixed synthetic outcomes. It must not be titled or framed as demonstrating that connectomic motifs generally fail, that biology lacks useful computation, or that the worm mechanism was independently validated.

The existing Route 2 report remains a narrower literature-grounded/synthetic study report. It does not replace the original four-result project and is not submission-ready. Changing journals or merging in unrelated M4–M10 contrasts would not resolve the contribution gap.

## What would materially change the decision

At least one of the following would need to occur before re-assessing an external research submission:

1. A genuinely new, independently supported biological computation and a pre-outcome AI test that isolates that computation against fair generic and resource-matched controls across an independent task family; or
2. A clearly novel and broadly important comparative-data conclusion that can be supported without pooling incompatible estimands, with an auditable evidence base and an independent contribution beyond the current bounded case records.

Prospective biological work remains deferred until a specific accessible lab, assay, independent unit and data path are verified. This is an operational condition. It is not a biological null and does not justify lowering the evidentiary bar.
