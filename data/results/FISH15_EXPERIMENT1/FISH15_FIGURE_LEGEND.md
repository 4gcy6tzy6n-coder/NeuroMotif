# Draft figure legend

**Fish1.5 recurrence–persistence analysis is non-supportive and its frozen graph null is mostly undefined.** (a) Each point represents one neuron from the single Fish1.5 specimen (82 neurons). Local recurrence index is the weighted two-step return count divided by the product of the neuron's within-cohort in-strength and out-strength. Post-offset persistence is the direction-balanced median trial ratio of baseline-subtracted mean dF/F at 60–70 s to that at 50–60 s, using the code-defined 0.5-s timebase and nominal 20–60-s stimulus. The association is Spearman ρ = −0.110; the frozen positive-direction label-permutation test gives p = 0.834. The paired neuron-bootstrap 95% percentile interval is [−0.293, 0.134] (9,844 of 10,000 resamples had a defined correlation). (b) Among 1,000 unique directed degree-preserving, globally weight-matched rewired graphs, 139 yielded a defined correlation and 861 yielded an undefined statistic because recurrence had zero variance. Therefore no topology-null p-value is estimable. This null does not preserve node-specific weighted strengths. All inferences are conditional on this one specimen; neurons are not independent animals. The analysis is retrospective and partially informed after a disclosed outcome-exposure incident. The primary positive association was not supported; the combined decision is indeterminate. No causal or population-level claim is made.

**Extended robustness panel.** Points show prespecified descriptive Spearman estimates for fixed robustness endpoints/cohorts; no intervals were computed for these secondary estimates. The expanded graph has 97 neurons. Sine phase lag and calibrated gain are unavailable from the released schema.
